The research draft remains a unified theoretical text. The shorter paper
remains self-contained and computer-free.

weakly integral anomaly citation and the matroid-bound typo, and adds a
provenance table. Its stronger computational rank, length and inventory

limitations, and clarifies attribution. Its main theorem is unchanged.

to the research draft: a perfect fusion-simple integral modular category of
dimension 5400 squared has rank at least 106, or 130 if dimension-900 simples
are absent. A twist order divisible by 30 gives the stronger bounds 112 and
142, respectively. The proof combines the completed pairwise twist graph
with the earlier full-graph degree budgets; it does not use the omitted
source-certificate archives.

Both endpoints remain unresolved. Arithmetic survivors are not fusion rings
or categories.
