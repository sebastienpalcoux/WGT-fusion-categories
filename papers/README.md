# Manuscripts

## Short, self-contained proofs

Each manuscript gives a complete proof of one theorem from the larger project. Readers can study either result on its own.

- **A rank bound for simple integral modular categories** (17 pages). Every non-pointed simple integral modular category has rank at least 32. The supplement gives the program and certificate for the final finite check. [PDF](https://github.com/sebastienpalcoux/WGT-fusion-categories/blob/main/papers/Rank32.pdf) · [TeX](https://github.com/sebastienpalcoux/WGT-fusion-categories/blob/main/papers/Rank32.tex) · [Verification supplement](rank-bound-supplement/README.md)
- **Weakly integral braided fusion categories below dimension $5400^2$** (29 pages). Such categories are weakly group-theoretical. Applying the theorem to the Drinfeld center gives the bound 5400 without a braiding assumption. The proof is computer-free. [PDF](https://github.com/sebastienpalcoux/WGT-fusion-categories/blob/main/papers/Below_5400_squared.pdf) · [TeX](https://github.com/sebastienpalcoux/WGT-fusion-categories/blob/main/papers/Below_5400_squared.tex)

The [full article](https://github.com/sebastienpalcoux/WGT-fusion-categories/blob/main/papers/Manuscript.pdf) contains the broader dimension and rank results, including the same general rank bound of 32 and the optimized search engine shared with the short paper, enhanced by the dimension-family exclusions proved in the full article. Its [release guide](../certificates/README.md) documents the associated computations.
