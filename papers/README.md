## Rank bounds

The three additions below substantially improve the **general rank-32 theorem of the 17-page paper to rank 45**. Their odd-dimensional bound, where included, is **217**, improving the earlier **57** in the 252-page Hexagon manuscript. All three are by Sebastian Burciu, Jingcheng Dong and Sébastien Palcoux.

| Manuscript | Pages | Main content | Files and companion |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| **A rank bound for simple integral modular data** | 24 | Theorem A, general case: rank at least **45**. | [PDF](rank-bounds/general/Rank_bound.pdf) · [TeX](rank-bounds/general/Rank_bound.tex) · [Guide](rank-bounds/general/README.md) |
| **Rank bounds for simple integral modular data** | 37 | Theorem A, general and odd-dimensional cases: ranks at least **45** and **217**. | [PDF](rank-bounds/general-and-odd/Rank_bounds.pdf) · [TeX](rank-bounds/general-and-odd/Rank_bounds.tex) · [Guide](rank-bounds/general-and-odd/README.md) |
| **The rank of simple integral modular data** — revised complete version | 98 | Ranks at least **45**, **217** in odd dimension, and **219** in odd nonsquare dimension; general theoretical restrictions; classification of nontrivial perfect weakly integral modular categories through rank 44. | [PDF](rank-bounds/full/Rank-modular-data.pdf) · [TeX](rank-bounds/full/Rank-modular-data.tex) · [Guide](rank-bounds/full/README.md) |
| **A rank bound for simple integral modular categories** | 17 | The original general bound **32**. The final finite check has its own original program and certificate. | [PDF](Rank32.pdf) · [TeX](Rank32.tex) · [Rank-32 companion](rank-bound-supplement/README.md) |

The category statements apply to **non-pointed simple integral modular fusion categories over ℂ**. The new papers formulate their main results algebraically for non-pointed based-simple commutative integral fusion rings with the specified symmetric Drinfeld duality and rotation-admissible cyclotomic modular data. The perfect-category classification in the complete paper has a different hypothesis from the simple-category rank theorem.

The [comparison page](rank-bounds/README.md) explains the choice of edition. Each edition retains its matching verification files. The full paper linked here is the revised version shared on 20 November 2026; the two extracts are the versions shared on 19 November 2026.
