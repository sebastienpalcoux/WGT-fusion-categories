# Pruning-to-proof map

This map specifies the mathematical contract of every family of exclusions in the production search. A TeX key below is the stable `\label` in `Rank-modular-data.tex`; equation keys identify displayed identities, and quoted paragraph titles identify their explicit implementation derivations. The map concerns completeness of the search, not categorifiability of a surviving degree list. Source hashes and repeated executions establish reproducibility; they do not replace these mathematical implications or a formal proof of the implementation.

## Standing assumptions and direction of every test

**A.** The input sought is non-pointed, based-simple, integral cyclotomic modular data with symmetric algebraic Drinfeld duality and rotation certificates, as defined in `alg:based`, `alg:drinfeld`, `alg:mod-data`, and `alg:rot-data`. In particular the unit is the only degree-one label. A general perfect datum is not an input to this search. Based simplicity is used in the coprime-entry vanishing, non-prime-power degree, strict character bound, degree-graph connectedness, and principal-block gcd restrictions. It cannot be dropped merely because an example is modular.

**S.** The square branch additionally has `D=M²`, even `M`, and the rank restriction stated at its entry point. Rank-specific profile tables and fixed-root exclusions are used only in their stated range. **N.** The nonsquare branch has `D=qM²`, square-free `q>1`, even `M`, and its stated rank restriction. **O.** The odd branch has odd dimension and pairs every nonunit label with its distinct dual. The duality and rank-congruence restrictions are proved in `alg:odd-parity`; they are not numerical assumptions about candidate lists.

Each failure below is a necessary-condition failure: its logical direction is **genuine datum ⇒ test passes**. The common floor, overlap, charge and closed-orbit accounting rule is `new:reservation-calculus`: simultaneous sorted floor vectors combine by coordinatewise maxima, undecided alternatives by minima, and at most h charges per label retain every hth charged floor starting with the largest. Closed-class semigroup credits require a Galois-stable eligible union; demands are added only when their positions are proved disjoint. A work, memory, denominator, or arithmetic-width cap must retain the branch, or transfer its exact state to arbitrary-precision enumeration. It must never mean mathematical rejection. No terminal test is asserted sufficient for fusion, modular, or categorical realization.

## Independent terminal witnesses and execution scope

`verification/independent/check.py` imports no production modules. It uses a
separate exhaustive tiling recursion and reconstructs the five terminal
conditions from the degree lists. Its `terminal-witnesses.jsonl.gz` records
one exact witness per covering-census row; `certificate.json` binds the
verifier and input archive and gives every table cell. The same checker
recomputes the odd starting counts, the final nonsquare tail and finite
group arithmetic. Its group-cohomology orbit counts assume the cohomology
groups and actions proved or cited in Section 10.

These terminal witnesses do not encode the complete native recursion tree.
Full coverage additionally uses the manuscript's structural reductions,
the producer's task partition and the source-bound completed executions.
The archive audit checks consistency and completion of the declared tasks;
it does not independently derive the task partition from mathematics.

`verification/native_boundary/check.py` compiles both native backends with
undefined-behavior detection, then repeats two positive near-terminal
fixtures with artificially small lcm ceilings. Every emitted handoff is
exhausted in Python and gives the same completed row set as the production
ceiling. `census/tests/test_driver_accounting.py` rejects truncated output,
nonzero exits, unexhausted or interrupted exact continuations and inconsistent
record counts. These are bounded protocol and arithmetic-transition tests.

`python3 verify.py --quick --independent --native-prefix` records the short
audit. It does not regenerate the denominator census or replay either
production odd certificate recursion. The full production odd replay remains
available as `python3 verify.py`; `--full` adds the auxiliary arithmetic
replays. A resource limit always leaves a candidate unresolved or transfers
its exact state; it does not certify mathematical exclusion.
