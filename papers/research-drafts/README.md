The draft records more than 30 rounds of work with GPT-6 on the family
$p^6q^6r^4$, including the unresolved dimension $5400^2$.
It develops the theoretical restrictions in one manuscript and identifies
which further numerical conclusions depend on complete computational
coverage that is not certified by this compact release.

The accompanying [compact checks](p6q6r4/verification/README.md) reproduce
specified bounded exact calculations. They do not replace the large
certificate inventories or establish that the remaining arithmetic states
are fusion rings or categories. Large verification archives are omitted;
they have not been split into parts. The entire companion, taken together, is below 10 MB.

See the [package guide](p6q6r4/README.md) for build, integrity and verification
commands and the theorem-to-check scope map.
