## The joint dimension, rank and decomposition project

**Prime factors of fusion-category dimensions: weak group-theoreticity, rank and decomposition** — 252 pages.

This is the broad manuscript recorded as **Hexagon 2611.00233**. It treats prime-exponent families, dimension thresholds, the earlier general and odd rank bounds 32 and 57, and decomposition results. The [29-page dimension proof](../Below_5400_squared.pdf) and [17-page rank-32 proof](../Rank32.pdf) offer focused entry points.

## The three-prime arithmetic frontier

**Arithmetic restrictions on braided fusion categories of dimension p⁶q⁶r⁴** — 423 pages.

The draft records more than 30 rounds of work with GPT-6 on this family, including the unresolved dimension **5400²**. Its theoretical discussion is unified in one manuscript. It distinguishes proved restrictions from stronger numerical conclusions whose complete computational coverage is not established by the compact release.

The [47-page computer-free paper](../three-prime-dimensions/three_prime_dimensions.pdf) gives a self-contained treatment of the main family results. The accompanying [compact checks](p6q6r4/verification/README.md) reproduce specified bounded exact calculations. They do not establish the omitted large inventories or turn surviving arithmetic data into categories.

## The complete rank investigation

**The rank of simple integral modular data** — revised complete version, 98 pages.

This later manuscript develops the stronger general and odd rank bounds **45 / 217**, with **219** in odd nonsquare dimension, and classifies nontrivial perfect weakly integral modular categories through rank 44. Its [24-page](../rank-bounds/general/README.md) and [37-page](../rank-bounds/general-and-odd/README.md) extracts isolate the principal rank theorems.

Only the revised complete version is supplied here. It is a separate development from the 252-page Hexagon manuscript, which remains the source of its explicitly versioned earlier references.

## Reading and checking the work

For both the dimension-bound and rank-bound investigations, the aim was to push the AI model as far as possible by repeatedly developing and testing stronger theoretical approaches. The focused papers make individual arguments easier to digest; the broader manuscripts record additional results and the limits reached so far.

Use the [verification guide](../../VERIFICATION.md) to select each manuscript's companion and the [version guide](../VERSIONS.md) for theorem numbering and Hexagon references.
